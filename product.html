El Paraiso

From the high altitudes of Huila, Colombia, El Paraiso is a testament to the dedication of local farmers. This single-origin coffee boasts a unique flavor profile, a result of meticulous cultivation and processing techniques. Experience the rich heritage and exceptional quality in every cup.

About the Farm

Nestled in the heart of Huila, El Paraiso farm is renowned for its commitment to sustainable practices and exceptional coffee quality. The farm's unique microclimate and rich volcanic soil contribute to the distinctive characteristics of this coffee. The farmers, with generations of experience, employ traditional methods combined with modern innovations to ensure the highest standards.

Tasting Notes

El Paraiso offers a captivating sensory experience. Expect a balanced cup with notes of dark chocolate, caramel, and a hint of citrus. The aroma is rich and inviting, with a smooth, lingering finish. This coffee is perfect for those who appreciate complexity and depth in their brew.

Brewing Suggestions

To fully appreciate the nuances of El Paraiso, we recommend a pour-over or French press brewing method. Use freshly ground beans and water at the optimal temperature (200°F). Experiment with different grind sizes and brewing times to find your perfect cup. For espresso lovers, this coffee delivers a rich and flavorful shot.
